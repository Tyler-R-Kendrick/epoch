# Design System: Assembly

## Philosophy
**"A release is a community's holiday, and its page should feel like the
eve of one."** Assembly is G4 at full volume: Epoch 13 as a monument
numeral, a name in quotation marks, a countdown, and a manifest where
every landed line traces back to the conversation it came from — lena's
idea, sam's wet-harbor field notes, an answer that became a guide. The
plaque grows in public; first-timers are counted; the one open slot is
framed as an invitation, not a gap. Underneath the ceremony the claims
are Epoch-true: the materialization is a point-in-time snapshot,
reproducible byte-for-byte, and this page becomes permanent when it
ships.

All ten round-3 candidates are screens of the same product,
Epoch.Community.Web (to Epoch what GitHub is to Git). Every candidate
must show Epoch primitives — intents, anchors, epochs, verified
identity, supervised agents, workspaces — as legible product concepts
with humane microcopy, and must serve the foundation goals G1–G5.

## Design System & Look
Cinematic dark editorial: near-black ground, thin rules, huge numerals,
generous space; amber as the epoch's celebratory metal; green only for
LANDED. No console cosplay — this is a keynote slide you can operate.

## Ideation
Rejected: changelog-first (a list is not a milestone) and tag-page-plus
(GitHub releases with paint). Chosen: monument + manifest + plaque —
the three things a community actually gathers around.

## Distinctness Check
The only dark candidate and the only monumental typography. Atrium
previews this epoch from home; the Gallery-style permanence lives in
the past-epochs rail ('open exhibition'); Pocket celebrates the same
ship on a phone.
