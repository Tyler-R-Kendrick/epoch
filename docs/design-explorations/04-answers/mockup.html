How do I keep episodes playing on a ferry with no signal?

asked yesterday 18:12by r. beck — first question, welcomed by nadia 412 views3 answers
RB

I commute across the sound twice a day. Ten minutes out, the signal dies and the episode dies with it. Is there a way to keep a few episodes on the phone — fetched at home, played on the water? On old phones too, ideally.

asked in #support · tagged offline, playback · anyone may answer — plain words welcome
✓ Accepted answer 44 found this helpful

Since Epoch 13 rc2 there's an offline shelf built into the tuner. It came from a proposal by another ferry commuter, so it does exactly what you're describing:

  1. Open Settings → Listening → Offline shelf and choose how many episodes to keep (default 5).
  2. Episodes fetch on wi-fi only, and the shelf caps itself on older phones — you'll see the cap stated in plain words.
  3. On the water, the player falls back to the shelf automatically. Nothing to press.
tuner ▸ shelf: keep 5 · fetch on wi-fi · cap 200 MB on this phone
