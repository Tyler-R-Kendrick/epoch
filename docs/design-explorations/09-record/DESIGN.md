# Design System: Record

## Philosophy
**"A contributor's history should read like a record of work, not a
slot machine."** Record is G2's identity surface: lena's page states
her kind of building without apology — citizen builder, zero pull
requests, two shipped features — and proves it with rows that each
terminate in permanent credit. Kinds are equal citizens: proposal, art,
copy, testing, docs, answers, founding. Reputation is quotation (mira's
sentence from the epoch notes), not karma. And the record is hers: the
Epoch substrate makes every row signed, exportable, and verifiable away
from this server, which the page says in exactly those words.

All ten round-3 candidates are screens of the same product,
Epoch.Community.Web (to Epoch what GitHub is to Git). Every candidate
must show Epoch primitives — intents, anchors, epochs, verified
identity, supervised agents, workspaces — as legible product concepts
with humane microcopy, and must serve the foundation goals G1–G5.

## Design System & Look
Luxe minimal document: ivory ground, large serif identity, hairline
rules, tabular record rows, burgundy as the single accent. No graphs,
no streaks, no badges-as-toys — restraint is the design statement.

## Ideation
Rejected: GitHub-style contribution graph (measures commits, erases
citizen builders) and gamified levels (cheapens the credit the product
exists to dignify). Chosen: the record-book — grouped by community,
stated in plain words, credited on epochs.

## Distinctness Check
Owns identity and the ivory/burgundy document register; the only
serif-led, single-accent, luxe-minimal candidate (Answers' serif is
functional reading; this is portraiture). Orbit finds a home; Record is
what you bring to it.
