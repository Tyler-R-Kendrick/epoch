# Nightboard marketing landing

Persuade-mode entry for the Nightboard exploration. Default URL (`index.html` /
`/`) is this surface. The Operate board lives at `board.html`.

**Job:** persuade a developer that Epoch Community is where they collaborate,
promote their work, and get paid as creators — then enter the board. Atmosphere
and motion embody Grid; they never replace the argument. Copy and CTA are ready
immediately — dawn theatre never blocks the decision.

**Do not market:** promoting chat messages into signed intents / receipt theater
as the product thesis. That is not the Persuade argument.

**Inspiration:** Tron / Nightboard Grid world; scroll-scrub ride patterned on
[shader.se](https://www.shader.se/) (fixed viewport + internal track — scroll
progress scrubs the world, not stacked document sections); premium coded
case-index from [aino.agency](https://aino.agency/) as `E00–E04` / `P01–P03`.
CRT tube immersion (WebGL barrel lens + chromatic aberration + phosphor/scan
stack; SVG displacement fallback) is landing-only material. Board Operate UI
stays character-first; CRT is Persuade-frame.

**Motion thesis:**
1. **Focal** — CRT tube + driving Tron floor + FIGlet wipe; CTA never gated.
2. **Continuity** — ride progress maps to chapters; floor/CRT energy lerps with
   `--nb-scroll-smooth`; case rail tracks chapter; one scene per gesture + settle.
3. **Interaction** — creator-loop theater scrub, plane catalog, keys `1`–`3`.
4. **Feedback** — case codes, phase tag, earn glow, canvas-fail recovery.

**Structure:** fixed `data-ride-stage` + tall `data-ride-track` spacer; chapters
are overlays activated by progress (`data-ride-chapter`).

**Primary action:** Enter the board (`board.html`).

**Constraints:** no fake social proof; no live payout claims (fixture earnings only).
Reduced motion: snap chapters, CRT warp off / low, no RAF lerp.
Orchestration: [`ORCHESTRATION.md`](ORCHESTRATION.md) · [`progress.html`](progress.html).
