E01 What it is

One place to collaborate on real work, promote what you ship, and get paid — instead of splitting that across chat, a forge, and a tip jar.

Built on Epoch, a signed, event-driven successor to Git, so community conversation and the work share one substrate.

E03 Who it is for

  • Creators — ship libraries, tools, games, docs, or design, and want that work visible and paid for.
  • Collaborators — build with others without bouncing between chat, forge, and social to finish a release.
  • Supporters — follow builders, cheer releases, and fund the creators they rely on.

E04 What’s on the board

Three surfaces you’ll use inside: rooms to collaborate, a feed to promote work, and linked projects for the code.
