# Marketing

## Target Customer

Continue targets individual developers who want an open-source assistant with flexible models and teams that need to create, share, govern, and run repeatable AI coding agents across repositories and integrations.

## Positioning

- Continue positions itself as a configurable agent platform rather than only an autocomplete extension.
- The product emphasizes Agent mode, Plan mode, Chat, Edit, Autocomplete, MCP tools, reusable rules, local and managed configs, team-private agents, and provider flexibility.
- Pricing language is increasingly oriented around agents, integrations, team control, SSO, BYOK, invoicing, and enterprise security.

## Customer Model

- Starter usage is token-metered for creating and running agents, integrations, and frontier-model credits.
- Team pricing adds shared private agents, control over which agents can be used, and SSO-style team login.
- Company pricing adds custom SSO, BYOK, commitment, invoicing, and SLA.

## Who They Capture

- Developers who want open-source IDE assistance without locking into one model provider.
- Teams that need reusable coding agents, shared rules, private catalogs, and governance.
- Organizations that prefer configuration-as-product over ad hoc local prompt conventions.

## Who They Miss

- Users who want a simple bundled subscription with minimal configuration.
- Developers frustrated by local-provider autocomplete quirks or config migration churn.
- Auditors who need immutable signed evidence rather than a mutable agent/config catalog.
