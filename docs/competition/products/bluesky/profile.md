# Bluesky

Bluesky is a **public social network on AT Protocol** with portable handles, custom feeds, and a soft sky-blue product UI. It is the friendlier timeline pole versus X, and the identity-adjacent social surface next to Tangled/Epoch federation work.

## Competitive Relevance

- Soft light UI and handle-forward identity are quality bars for modern social products.
- Custom feeds show how algorithm choice can be chrome without becoming Slack-level complexity.
- Epoch should steal approachable surfaces and visible identity, not pure social feed economics.
