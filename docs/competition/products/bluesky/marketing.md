# Marketing

## Target Customers

- Users seeking a less hostile public square with portable identity.
- Developers and communities already in the ATProto ecosystem.

## Positioning

Open social networking with user choice of algorithms/feeds and portable accounts.

## Customer Model

Free consumer app; ecosystem growth via protocol; evolving monetization.

## Excluded / Weak Fit

- Enterprise channel workspaces.
- Signed repository change management as primary UX.
