# Cursor Bugbot Marketing

## Target Customer

Cursor targets developers and teams already using Cursor as the main AI coding environment. Bugbot specifically targets teams whose agent-authored or Cursor-authored pull requests need a first-pass reviewer before humans spend attention.

## Positioning

- Bugbot is positioned as AI code review for pull requests, focused on bugs, security, and quality rather than style-only comments.
- Cursor frames the May 2026 change as removing seat fees and letting teams pay based on usage and review effort.
- The broader Cursor story is generation plus validation plus fix loops in one developer workspace.

## Customer Model

- New users get a free trial path.
- Teams and individuals are moving to usage-based Bugbot billing, with average review costs advertised around one to one and a half dollars depending on PR size and complexity.
- Cursor's enterprise story inherits team management, usage controls, and security partnerships from the larger Cursor platform.

## Who They Capture

- Cursor-first teams that want review findings to flow directly into the editor.
- Developers who value automatic PR review but want manual triggers and effort controls.
- Teams worried about AI-generated code introducing bugs and vulnerable dependencies.

## Who They Miss

- Non-Cursor teams that do not want editor lock-in.
- Organizations that need forge-neutral, signed review evidence.
- Power users who feel usage billing discourages iterative review on every small update.
