# Marketing

## Target Customers

- Individual developers who want a visual Git client and richer repository history than a terminal workflow.
- Teams managing many repositories, PRs, issues, and build signals across multiple hosting and planning systems.
- Engineering leaders buying a DevEx platform with GitLens, desktop workflow, Launchpad, DORA insight, AI, MCP, SSO, and admin controls.

## Positioning

GitKraken positions itself as a Git productivity and DevEx platform. The message is not "replace your forge"; it is "keep your existing Git hosts and make daily work visible, faster, and more coordinated."

## Customer Model

- Free Community plan for OSS and public repositories.
- Paid Pro, Advanced, and Business tiers for private repos, team features, self-hosted integrations, SSO, AI controls, cloud workspaces, Launchpad, and support.
- On-premise options target enterprises that want license control or air-gapped deployment.

## Captures

- Developers who want visual confidence before rebases, merges, resets, and conflict resolution.
- Multi-repo teams that feel fragmented across GitHub, GitLab, Bitbucket, Azure DevOps, Jira, and Trello.
- Managers who want work-in-progress and PR readiness without building custom dashboards.

## Misses

- Teams unwilling to pay for private-repository features.
- Security-sensitive organizations that reject cloud-backed patches, AI, telemetry, or account-dependent collaboration.
- Developers who prefer simple terminal-first tools and do not want another desktop state model.
