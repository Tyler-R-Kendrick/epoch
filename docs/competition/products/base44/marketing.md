# Base44 Marketing

## Positioning

Base44 positions itself as a no-code AI app builder for turning business ideas into working apps with prompts. Its strongest message is speed plus managed completeness: frontend, backend, auth, database, integrations, privacy, access control, and publish flow in one product.

## Target Customers

- Small-business operators and solo founders who want custom tools without hiring engineers.
- Internal-tool builders who need auth, data, forms, payments, scheduling, and SaaS connectors.
- Non-technical product owners who value one-click publish over repository-native control.
- Wix-adjacent customers who already believe in hosted creation platforms.

## Customer Model

- Free entry point with limited credits.
- Paid monthly plans increase AI message credits, integration credits, and platform capabilities.
- Builder and higher tiers target serious app creators with GitHub integration, backend functions, domains, and more capacity.
- Wix benefits from converting vibe-coding demand into premium subscriptions and business-solution expansion.

## Who They Capture

- Users who are blocked more by app assembly than by code editing.
- Buyers who want a managed business-app stack and do not want to choose Supabase, Auth0, Vercel, GitHub Actions, and integration middleware separately.
- Creators who accept credit metering if the platform removes setup work.

## Who They Miss

- Teams that require deterministic repository ownership of backend logic.
- Developers who need local-first debugging, full source export, explicit dependency boundaries, and stable CI gates.
- Regulated or production-heavy teams that need contractual uptime, change windows, audit trails, and rollback evidence.
- Builders whose apps depend on integrations that consume credits unpredictably.

## Epoch Positioning Lesson

Base44 wins by collapsing complexity for non-coders. Epoch should counter-position around durable ownership, signed change evidence, and recoverable history for teams that outgrow opaque managed builders.
