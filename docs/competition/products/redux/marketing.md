# Marketing

## Target Customers

- Front-end and full-stack teams building medium-to-large single-page apps that
  outgrew component-local state.
- Engineers who want an explicit, testable, and inspectable state model with a
  clear action trail.
- Teams standardizing a shared state convention across many contributors and
  long-lived codebases.
- Developers who value a large ecosystem of middleware, devtools, and
  integrations over an all-in-one product.

## Positioning

Redux positions itself as a predictable state container and, through Redux
Toolkit, as "the official, opinionated, batteries-included toolset for efficient
Redux development." The promise is predictability, debuggability, and a standard
pattern rather than a hosted product or collaboration engine.

## Customer Model

- Pure open-source adoption through npm, documentation, tutorials, and the RTK
  quick-start funnel.
- No direct monetization: value capture is ecosystem gravity, employability, and
  a hiring signal ("Redux experience") rather than a paid SaaS.
- The brand is reinforced by being the default answer in tutorials, job posts,
  and framework starter kits.

## Captured Audiences

- Teams that need a well-understood, heavily documented state pattern with strong
  TypeScript support.
- Engineers who rely on Redux DevTools time-travel to debug complex state
  transitions.
- Apps with substantial derived state, middleware pipelines, or async data flows
  managed through RTK Query.
- Organizations that prize convention and onboarding speed across large teams.

## Missed Or Under-Captured Audiences

- Teams that need real-time collaboration, presence, or multi-client convergence
  out of the box rather than as bolt-on libraries.
- Products that require durable, signed, or auditable history rather than an
  in-memory action log that disappears on refresh.
- Apps wanting first-class offline-first sync without assembling persistence,
  offline queueing, and conflict handling themselves.
- Developers seeking lighter-weight stores who have migrated toward Zustand,
  Jotai, or signals for local state.
