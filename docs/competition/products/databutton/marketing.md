# Marketing

## Target Customer

Databutton targets non-technical founders, businesses, and teams that want AI to build real internal applications, SaaS products, and AI workflows but still need an expert path when the build gets serious.

## Positioning

- Databutton calls itself the world's first fully AI app developer.
- The product emphasizes shipping ideas in days, not weeks, with AI planning, research, code, and deployment.
- The marketing is unusually explicit that the user remains in the cockpit and must provide vision and expertise.
- Human-support packages position Databutton as a tech partner rather than a purely self-serve SaaS tool.

## Customer Model

- Agent plus Community is a lower-cost AI-first plan with community support and office hours.
- Agent plus Human support adds a dedicated Slack channel, app porting, expert help, and meetings.
- Agent plus Human advisor adds CTO-style planning, expert feature completion, weekly syncs, and higher-touch collaboration.
- Additional seats and teams are handled through contact-based pricing.

## Who They Capture

- Businesses that want an AI builder but are willing to pay for expert rescue or guidance.
- Non-technical founders who need a full-stack app without hiring a full team upfront.
- Teams building AI apps that use APIs, knowledge bases, workflows, and personalization.
- Customers who prefer Python backend code over opaque no-code logic.

## Who They Miss

- Users looking for a cheap, purely self-serve app builder.
- Engineering teams that want full local repository control from the first commit.
- Regulated buyers that need independent evidence of every agent and human action.
- Teams that do not want production delivery bundled with vendor support.
