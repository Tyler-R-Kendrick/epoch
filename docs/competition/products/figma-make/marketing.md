# Marketing

## Target Customer

Figma Make targets designers, product managers, founders, marketers, and design-led teams that already work in Figma and want to turn ideas into functional prototypes or web apps without waiting for a development handoff.

## Positioning

- Figma positions Make as the path from idea or design file to working app.
- The copy emphasizes prompts, design-system context, high-fidelity prototypes, direct edits, Supabase data, Figma Sites, and the broader Figma AI suite.
- The message is not "replace engineering" as much as "make ideas real enough to test and align around."

## Customer Model

- Figma Make sits inside the Figma account, seat, plan, and AI-credit model.
- Advanced capabilities such as Make kits, connectors, organization controls, and admin permissions are tied to paid or organization-level plans.
- Supabase and connector integrations extend the product from design workflow into app-data and workplace-context workflows.

## Who They Capture

- Design teams that want to explore working UI without leaving Figma.
- Product teams that want executable prototypes before committing engineering capacity.
- Enterprises that already manage design systems, variables, libraries, and permissions in Figma.
- Teams using Notion, Linear, GitHub, Atlassian, and similar tools as product context sources.

## Who They Miss

- Developers who prefer starting from the repository, local tests, and CI rather than design files.
- Teams outside the Figma ecosystem.
- Buyers who need deterministic cost and generation records for every model call.
- Organizations that cannot let MCP connectors expose internal workspaces to AI generation without independent evidence.
