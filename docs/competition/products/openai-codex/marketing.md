# OpenAI Codex Marketing

## Target Customer

OpenAI markets Codex to individual developers, open-source maintainers, professional engineering teams, and enterprises already adopting ChatGPT or OpenAI models. The 2026 app positioning reaches beyond pair programming toward developers supervising multiple agents across design, build, ship, and maintenance work.

## Positioning

- "Command center for agents" is the core message.
- Codex is framed as one system across app, CLI, IDE, web/cloud tasks, GitHub review, and skills.
- OpenAI emphasizes parallel work, long-running tasks, sandboxing, approvals, and extensibility through skills.

## Customer Model

- Codex access is bundled into ChatGPT plans for Plus, Pro, Business, Enterprise, and Edu users.
- Most customers now see Codex metered through credits tied to input, cached input, and output token usage.
- Enterprise value is governance, scale, managed policy, and the ability to extend Codex through skills and internal workflows.

## Who It Captures

- Developers who already trust ChatGPT and want a first-party coding agent.
- Teams that want local and cloud agents without buying a separate coding-agent vendor.
- Organizations willing to make OpenAI a central agent runtime and usage-metering layer.

## Who It Misses

- Teams that require vendor-neutral, self-hosted, or airgapped change history.
- Buyers who need predictable per-seat pricing for large autonomous-agent workloads.
- Maintainers who want signed provenance as a repository-native primitive rather than a vendor task log.

## Epoch Opportunity

Epoch should avoid competing as another agent surface. The opening is durable agent provenance: signed snapshots, verifiable review context, and content-addressed evidence that survives outside Codex threads and credit ledgers.
