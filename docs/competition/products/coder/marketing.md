# Coder Marketing

## Target Customer

- Enterprises that need secure remote development without surrendering source code or infrastructure control.
- Platform engineering teams standardizing workspaces across large developer populations.
- Regulated industries, including finance, government, technology, and automotive.
- Organizations experimenting with self-hosted or network-isolated AI coding agents.

## Positioning

Coder positions itself as self-hosted cloud development infrastructure for developers and their agents. The pitch is consistency, security, compliance, faster onboarding, and AI governance in customer-controlled infrastructure.

## Customer Model

- Open-source community usage for teams comfortable operating the platform.
- Premium enterprise features, support, and governance for large organizations.
- Template registry and integrations create a platform ecosystem around workspaces.
- Sales-led positioning for teams needing air-gapped, network-isolated, or regulated deployments.

## Captured Audiences

- Teams that reject fully hosted SaaS for source-code or compliance reasons.
- DevOps teams already standardized on Terraform and Kubernetes.
- Enterprises trying to give AI agents controlled infrastructure rather than laptop-level credentials.

## Missed Audiences

- Small teams that want a managed sandbox without operating a control plane.
- Designers, students, and lightweight web developers who value instant browser demos over infrastructure ownership.
- Teams whose main need is signed repository provenance rather than workspace orchestration.
