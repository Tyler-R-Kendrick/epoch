# Marketing

## Target Customers

- Mid-market and enterprise teams replacing email for internal coordination.
- Organizations that want app integrations (CI, tickets, bots) inside chat.

## Positioning

"Where work happens" — the digital HQ for channels, threads, and automation.

## Customer Model

Seat-based SaaS with free tier limits; value concentrated in paid search, retention, and enterprise controls.

## Excluded / Weak Fit

- Public social audiences.
- Fully local-first or cryptographically accountable collaboration as core promise.
