# Gossip

## What People Say

Positive: channels beat email chaos; search (paid) is excellent; integrations are deep.

Skeptical: notification fatigue; pricing; feature sprawl; "Slack is where work goes to die" jokes about unread debt.

## Bug And Friction Themes

- Too many surfaces (canvases, lists, huddles, tabs) diluting the channel core.
- Performance at large workspace scale.
- Mobile parity lag for power features.
