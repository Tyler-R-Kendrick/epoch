# Discord

Discord is the default **community messaging** platform for many developer teams, open-source projects, and product tribes. Its hierarchy is **server (community) → channels → messages**, deliberately independent of source-control remotes. That independence is why teams keep Discord *and* GitHub: hangout, support, and culture do not fit inside a single repository UI.

## Competitive Relevance

- Proves that **dev social home ≠ repository**.
- Multi-community switcher + per-community channel lists are the UX bar for “I belong here.”
- Epoch’s prior dual-plane (global Dev Feed → repo channels) still under-served community-native conversation.
- Epoch should own **signed communities** with channels *and* linked repos — not Discord clones, not repo-only shells.
