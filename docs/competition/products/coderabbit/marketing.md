# CodeRabbit Marketing

## Target Customer

CodeRabbit markets to software teams that have already adopted GitHub, GitLab, Azure DevOps, Bitbucket, Jira, Linear, IDEs, CLIs, and increasingly AI coding agents. The strongest fit is a team whose pull request volume is rising and whose human reviewers need context-aware first-pass feedback.

## Positioning

- "AI code review" is the entry point, but current positioning expands into planning, agent handoff, IDE review, CLI review, Slack, and workflow analytics.
- The product claims value through faster shipping, context-aware review, standards enforcement, and review findings that can be fixed automatically.
- Pro and Pro Plus pricing frame CodeRabbit as a per-developer productivity layer, while Enterprise adds security review, procurement, governance, and custom limits.

## Customer Model

- Free plan and trials capture individual developers and open-source/private repo experimentation.
- Paid teams buy per user and manage rolling review quotas or usage-based overage.
- Enterprise customers buy procurement, security, compliance, rate-limit customization, and deeper platform integration.

## Who They Capture

- Teams drowning in AI-generated pull requests.
- Engineering managers who want review analytics and standardized gates.
- Developers who want review feedback before opening a PR from IDE or CLI surfaces.

## Who They Miss

- Teams unwilling to retain review knowledge in a SaaS system.
- Small teams that experience per-user or overage pricing as too much for occasional PR review.
- Organizations that need repository-native, content-addressed review evidence rather than platform-stored learnings.
