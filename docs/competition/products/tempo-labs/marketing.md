# Tempo Labs Marketing

## Positioning

Tempo positions around AI-assisted UI and React app building for teams that care about design quality. The message is not only "build from a prompt"; it is closer to "turn planned product and design intent into maintainable React faster."

Sources:

- [Tempo Labs YC profile](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/tempo-labs)
- [Tempo Labs review and pricing](https://vibecompare.dev/tools/tempo-labs/)
- [Tempo community welcome](https://www.reddit.com/r/Tempolabs/comments/1i6kkev)

## Target Customers

- Product teams building multi-page React apps.
- Designers and engineers trying to reduce Figma-to-code handoff loss.
- Startup teams that want a structured AI builder rather than a loose prompt surface.
- Developers who want GitHub-visible output while still using visual editing.

## Customer Model

- Freemium credit entry for experimentation.
- Pro and high-priced team/agent tiers for heavier usage and collaboration.
- The enterprise signal is stronger than pure hobbyist app builders because design systems, GitHub, and planning are part of the value proposition.

## Who They Capture

- Teams that have design assets and want React code without starting from blank prompts.
- Builders who were burned by fast AI builders that generate poor UI structure.
- Product owners who need the design review loop to stay visible.

## Who They Miss

- Users who need a complete backend, database, auth, integration, and deployment platform in one place.
- Non-design-led builders who want the fastest possible MVP, not a planning workflow.
- Teams outside React/Tailwind-style frontend stacks.
- Buyers who require proven import/export reliability before adopting a design-to-code platform.

## Epoch Positioning Lesson

Tempo's marketing shows that "AI builder" is fragmenting into roles. Epoch can position below these tools as durable product history: design intent, code generation, review, and runtime evidence should all become auditable changes.
