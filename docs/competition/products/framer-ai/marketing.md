# Marketing

## Target Customer

Framer AI markets to designers, startups, agencies, marketers, content teams, students, portfolio builders, and enterprises that want high-quality websites with fast publishing and collaborative editing.

## Positioning

- Framer positions AI as a way to avoid the blank canvas while preserving designer taste and control.
- The platform emphasizes design, publish, collaborate, CMS, SEO, hosting, performance, updates, and enterprise support.
- On-Page Editing is positioned as a way for anyone to make safe website updates without design-canvas or CMS bottlenecks.

## Customer Model

- Free usage supports non-commercial exploration.
- Basic targets personal and small professional sites with AI-powered design tools, custom domains, hosting, and SEO.
- Pro and Scale add staging, rollback, roles, permissions, relational CMS, redirects, advanced traffic needs, localization add-ons, and usage scaling.
- Enterprise sells custom limits, annual billing, security, and dedicated support.

## Who They Capture

- Design-led teams that want AI acceleration without leaving a visual site builder.
- Marketing organizations that need CMS, SEO, localization, publishing, and content editing.
- Agencies and startups that value polished presentation more than full-stack application logic.

## Who They Miss

- Engineering teams that need source-control-native app history, tests, signed builds, and infrastructure-neutral deployments.
- Products with complex application state, backend workflows, or regulated transaction logic.
- Organizations that require every generated content and design change to map to a repository commit and approval artifact.

## Epoch Takeaway

Framer AI makes website operations faster, but Epoch can own the deeper evidence layer: who generated, edited, reviewed, localized, and published each product-facing change.
