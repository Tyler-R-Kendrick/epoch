# Gossip

## Positive Sentiment

- Framer is perceived as a high-quality design-led website builder, and AI features benefit from that existing brand.
- On-Page Editing received positive positioning because it reduces bottlenecks between content, marketing, and design teams.
- Framer leadership publicly emphasizes taste and quality, which aligns with professional designer concerns about generic AI output.

## Complaints And Friction

- The same leadership commentary highlights the category risk: AI makes it easy to produce low-quality generic work quickly.
- Site pricing, add-ons, extra editors, localization, traffic, and enterprise needs can make the real cost higher than the entry plan suggests.
- Framer's hosted model is convenient but can frustrate teams that want code ownership or repository-native deployment.

## Bugs And Product Risk

- Marketing-site AI can produce visually convincing pages that still need brand, accessibility, SEO, localization, legal, and performance review.
- Letting non-designers edit live pages requires strong review and rollback discipline to avoid brand drift.
- AI Translate and generated content can create localization errors that are hard to catch without native-language review.

## Epoch Opportunity

Epoch can learn from Framer's collaboration loop while offering stronger provenance for AI-created website changes, translation decisions, content approvals, and publish events.
