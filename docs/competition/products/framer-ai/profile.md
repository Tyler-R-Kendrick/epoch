# Framer AI

Framer AI is an AI-assisted website creation and editing surface inside Framer's design, CMS, hosting, and collaboration platform. It competes with Epoch where generated product and marketing surfaces need design fidelity, live publishing, contributor workflows, localization, and controlled content changes.

## Competitive Relevance

- Framer AI includes Wireframer for prompt-to-responsive-page starts, Workshop for no-code component creation, AI Translate for site localization, and AI plugin extensibility.
- Framer's broader platform includes design, publishing, collaboration, CMS, SEO, hosting, performance, updates, templates, components, and marketplace assets.
- Pricing bundles AI-powered design tools into paid site plans, with Basic, Pro, Scale, and Enterprise tiers.
- On-Page Editing lets non-designers edit live site content without opening the design canvas or CMS, with review workflows.

## Epoch Implications

- Framer shows how AI-assisted generation blends with mature hosted publishing and content workflows.
- Epoch can differentiate by making marketing-site changes, generated components, localization, CMS edits, and publish events auditable outside a single hosted website builder.
- Framer's designer-led stance highlights that AI speed does not replace taste, governance, and brand-specific review.

## Unknowns To Track

- AI feature limits, add-on entitlements, and enterprise controls should be rechecked before quoting.
- Framer is primarily a site and marketing platform; comparisons to full app builders should distinguish content sites from application runtimes.
