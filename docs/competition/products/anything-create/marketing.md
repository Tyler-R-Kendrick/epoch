# Anything Create Marketing

## Positioning

Anything positions itself as a broad AI app builder: turn words into sites and apps, then publish and scale without writing code. It markets creativity, speed, mobile/web reach, AI integrations, export, custom domains, automated testing, visual QA, and advanced agents.

## Target Customers

- Non-technical founders and creators who want to turn app ideas into web or mobile products.
- Indie builders and small teams that want app generation, hosting, and integrations without configuring the stack.
- Higher-tier buyers who want autonomous agents, automated testing, visual QA, and priority support.
- Teams willing to pay for credits if generation, repair, and publish flow reduce engineering dependency.

## Customer Model

- Free plan with monthly and daily credits.
- Paid Pro tier adds private projects, custom domains, branding removal, and more credits.
- Higher tier adds Max agent capability, multiple agents, automated testing, visual QA, connection checks, and priority support.
- Credits are consumed by builder messages and by AI integrations inside published apps.

## Who They Capture

- Users who value broad app creation over repository-native development.
- Creators who want web, mobile, publishing, and AI integrations in one platform.
- Builders who are attracted by automated QA as a substitute for manual engineering review.

## Who They Miss

- Developers who want local control, deterministic builds, and complete repository-first workflow.
- Buyers who are skeptical of credit-based repair loops.
- Production teams that need a formal release process, test artifacts, SLAs, observability, and explicit data migration history.
- Users who experienced rebrand, migration, cancellation, or refund friction.

## Epoch Positioning Lesson

Anything raises the bar by making generated testing part of app-builder marketing. Epoch should answer with inspectable proof: test scope, signed results, accepted changes, and rollback points rather than opaque "verified" claims.
