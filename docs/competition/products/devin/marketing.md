# Marketing

## Target Customer

Devin targets engineering teams that want to delegate complete software tasks rather than only accelerate typing. The clearest buyers are AI-forward product engineering organizations, platform teams with repeatable maintenance work, and enterprises willing to configure agent environments for private repositories.

## Positioning

- Cognition positions Devin as the first autonomous software engineer.
- The product narrative is work completion: analyze the codebase, use tools, run tests, open PRs, respond to feedback, and automate recurring events.
- Skills, GitHub integration, automations, and review flows position Devin as a managed engineering worker rather than a model wrapper.

## Customer Model

- Self-serve and team usage is oriented around session or agent-compute consumption rather than only fixed editor seats.
- Enterprise value comes from private repositories, custom environments, SSO/security, dedicated support, and integration into existing engineering operations.
- The buyer must budget not just for model use but for review time, environment setup, policy, and failure handling.

## Who They Capture

- Teams with well-scoped backlog items, migration tasks, dependency upgrades, CI fixes, and reviewable maintenance work.
- Managers who want to convert tickets or operational events into agent labor.
- Organizations that can invest in blueprints, skills, access controls, and review procedures.

## Who They Miss

- Developers who want tight local edit loops with low latency.
- Teams that cannot grant hosted agents repository, dependency, browser, or secret access.
- Safety-sensitive organizations that require independent signed provenance rather than vendor-managed session records.
