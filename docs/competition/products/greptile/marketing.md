# Greptile Marketing

## Target Customer

Greptile markets to teams that already feel the pain of fast AI-authored code and slow human review. Its strongest target is a professional engineering organization with enough pull request volume to justify a dedicated AI review agent and enough codebase complexity to need cross-file context.

## Positioning

- Greptile presents itself as "The AI Code Reviewer" and a "central validation layer" for code generated by humans or coding agents.
- The proof strategy relies on customer logos, named bug examples, testimonials, and claims about catching issues humans miss.
- Enterprise positioning is security-forward: self-hosting, SOC 2, audit logs, SSO, GitHub Enterprise, and air-gapped deployment.

## Customer Model

- Pro: per-seat monthly pricing with included review volume and per-review overage.
- Open source: free for qualified non-commercial projects with common OSS licenses.
- Startup discount: early-stage companies can apply for a discount.
- Enterprise: custom terms, self-hosting, support, compliance, and procurement.

## Who They Capture

- Teams that want deep review quality more than cheapest possible LLM comments.
- Organizations standardizing on coding agents and needing one validation layer across them.
- Security-conscious customers that want self-hosted review infrastructure.

## Who They Miss

- High-PR-volume small teams that are sensitive to per-review overage.
- Teams skeptical of benchmark-style quality claims or unwilling to trust a vendor's repository graph.
- Users who prefer review evidence and policy to live directly in signed repository artifacts.
