# Marketing

## Target Customers

- Developers who understand DVCS pain deeply and are interested in mathematically grounded alternatives.
- Open source users dissatisfied with rebasing, conflict recurrence, and Git's snapshot model.
- Users who want partial clones and clean composition of independent changes.

## Positioning

Pijul positions itself as a free, open source DVCS based on a sound theory of patches, with simpler workflows and stronger merge properties.

## Customer Model

Open source project and community infrastructure rather than commercial SaaS.

## Captures

- VCS researchers and systems-minded developers.
- Users attracted to Darcs-like patch workflows with better performance goals.

## Misses

- Teams that need broad hosting, integrations, CI, and enterprise support.
- Developers unwilling to leave Git's ecosystem.
- Users who need strong visual onboarding or a polished collaboration UI.
