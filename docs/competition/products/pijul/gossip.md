# Gossip

## What People Say

Pijul's concept is respected, but public discussions show recurring anxiety about maturity, performance, and bugs.

## Bug And Friction Themes

- Recent Nest discussions include reports titled around broken beta releases, clone bugs, local config problems, reset surprises, and unresolved conflict markers after a fresh clone.
- Older public forum discussion reported severe performance and repository-size issues compared with Git on moderate workloads.
- The product's own theory is a strength, but the ecosystem must prove day-to-day reliability before teams will migrate.

## Product Risk For Epoch

Epoch should not rely on advanced merge theory alone as a selling point. The practical proof must be: fast enough, reliable enough, and easy to explain under real collaboration pressure.
