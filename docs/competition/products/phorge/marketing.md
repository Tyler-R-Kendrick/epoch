# Marketing

## Target Customers

- Existing Phabricator users that need a maintained upstream.
- Open-source projects and self-hosting teams that want an integrated code review, repository, task, wiki, and project portal.
- Organizations willing to adopt an opinionated review workflow in exchange for data ownership and deep integration.

## Positioning

Phorge positions itself as community-driven continuity for Phabricator: open source, opinionated, self-owned, and integrated. The message is less commercial growth and more "keep this proven development suite alive."

## Customer Model

- Open-source community project rather than SaaS subscription business.
- Adoption is infrastructure-led: teams install or migrate an instance, then contributors use the instance's workflows.
- Commercial value is mostly indirect through hosting, consulting, internal maintenance, or organizations supporting the fork.

## Captures

- Communities with existing Phabricator data and workflows that do not want a disruptive migration.
- Teams that need pre-push review, post-publish audit, tasks, wiki, repository browser, and automation in one self-hosted portal.
- Users who value ownership of data over vendor-managed cloud features.

## Misses

- Teams that want mainstream GitHub/GitLab pull-request conventions.
- Buyers who require a single accountable commercial vendor and enterprise roadmap.
- Windows-heavy development organizations or teams that expect modern IDE-native collaboration out of the box.
