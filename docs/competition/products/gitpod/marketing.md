# Marketing

## Target Customer

Gitpod markets to enterprise platform, security, and developer productivity teams that want standardized development environments without placing source code and secrets in a vendor-hosted workspace.

## Positioning

- Zero-trust platform for automated development environments.
- Run in the customer's cloud, VPC, on-premises infrastructure, or local desktop.
- Built for human and agentic developers as software development becomes more automated.
- Seat-based budgeting plus reuse of existing infrastructure.

## Customer Model

Gitpod captures organizations moving from ad hoc local setup and older CDE pilots toward a governed environment platform. The commercial model is enterprise adoption around runners, organizations, projects, secrets, audit logs, support, and rollout governance.

## Who They Capture

- Enterprises with strict source-code residency requirements.
- Teams that want repeatable Dev Container-based onboarding.
- Platform groups standardizing setup, database seeding, tests, and environment automations.
- Security buyers worried about AI agents increasing development-environment risk.

## Who They Do Not Capture

- Developers looking for a standalone AI coding assistant.
- Small teams that prefer hosted Codespaces-style convenience over runner administration.
- Buyers whose main need is immutable signed provenance across tools rather than controlled environment provisioning.

## Epoch Opportunity

Gitpod's pitch starts with where code runs. Epoch's pitch should start with how work becomes durable, signed, and reviewable across environments after it runs.
