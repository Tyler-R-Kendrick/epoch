# Marketing

## Target Customers

- Bazel teams that need managed remote cache, remote execution, results UI, and build/test debugging.
- Platform and developer-productivity teams responsible for build speed, CI cost, and reproducible execution environments.
- Enterprises with large monorepos, iOS/macOS needs, custom executors, SSO/SAML, SLAs, or on-prem requirements.

## Positioning

BuildBuddy positions itself as "Enterprise Bazel": a managed platform that brings Bazel performance and visibility to organizations without forcing every team to operate remote cache, execution, and results infrastructure themselves.

## Customer Model

- Personal tier for individual developers, small teams, and open-source projects.
- Team tier with pay-as-you-go cache transfer and larger remote-build capacity.
- Enterprise tier with SSO/SAML, isolated infrastructure, unlimited RBE cores, dedicated support, SLAs, and on-prem deployment.

## Captures

- Teams already committed to Bazel that need cache and execution reliability quickly.
- Build engineers who want UI-backed answers for cache misses, flaky tests, slow actions, and remote-execution behavior.
- Organizations that want managed Bazel expertise rather than building every internal platform component.

## Misses

- Teams not ready to adopt Bazel or REAPI concepts.
- Small projects where CI time is not painful enough to justify a specialized platform.
- Security-sensitive teams that need strong guarantees about build-event retention, source access, executor isolation, and telemetry before adopting cloud-hosted execution.
