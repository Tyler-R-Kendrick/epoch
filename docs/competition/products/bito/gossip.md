# Bito Gossip

## What People Like

- Developers like review agents when they catch routine issues and hand obvious fixes back to an implementing agent.
- Bito's Git provider coverage, CLI handoff, and agent-rule setup make it easier to insert into existing workflows than a new IDE.
- Review analytics and custom rules appeal to managers trying to understand whether AI review is reducing burden or creating noise.

## Repeated Complaints

- Experienced developers remain skeptical that AI reviewers catch deep logic errors rather than pattern-level issues.
- PR comment overload is a common complaint across AI review tools; teams want separate views, better filtering, and fewer notifications.
- Some reviewers worry about a loop where AI writes code, AI reviews it, and humans lose track of the real design risk.

## Bugs And Friction

- Codebase-aware review quality depends on indexing, rules, Jira linkage, and the clarity of the pull request.
- Learned rules can reduce noise, but they also create another governance surface that maintainers must inspect.
- Analytics may help optimize review workflows, but they do not prove correctness or capture every missed defect.

## Epoch Takeaway

Bito shows that review is becoming a separate AI market. Epoch should preserve the evidence Bito-style tools produce and make the accepted review trail independently auditable.
