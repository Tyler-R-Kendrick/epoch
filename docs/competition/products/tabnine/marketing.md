# Tabnine Marketing

## Target Customer

Tabnine markets to enterprise software organizations, regulated industries, security-conscious engineering leaders, and platform teams that need AI coding assistance without giving up control of code, models, deployment location, or compliance posture.

## Positioning

- "AI that you control" remains the central brand promise.
- The 2026 positioning elevates the Enterprise Context Engine as the context layer for reliable enterprise agents.
- Tabnine frames generic code generation as commoditized and positions organizational intelligence as the differentiator.

## Customer Model

- Public pricing emphasizes paid team and enterprise plans rather than a hobbyist-first funnel.
- Enterprise value comes from SaaS, VPC, on-premises, or air-gapped deployment choices; zero-retention privacy; SSO; compliance; and broader agentic workflows.
- Agent and Context Engine features create an expansion path beyond autocomplete seats.

## Who It Captures

- Buyers in finance, healthcare, government, and large enterprises where procurement and security approval matter as much as model quality.
- Teams with legacy or multi-repo systems where generic code assistants produce plausible but architecture-breaking code.
- Organizations that want AI coding help but do not want one foundation-model vendor to own the full developer workflow.

## Who It Misses

- Individual developers looking for a cheap or free personal coding assistant.
- Teams that prefer the fastest frontier-model coding agent over controlled deployment.
- Organizations that want repository-native signed provenance rather than an AI platform's internal context and session history.

## Epoch Opportunity

Epoch should treat Tabnine as the privacy and enterprise-control benchmark. The differentiator is not claiming better context; it is making accepted AI-assisted changes portable, signed, and auditable across tools.
