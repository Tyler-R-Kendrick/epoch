# Marketing

## Target Customers

- Consumers, large public channels, power users who want speed and cross-device sync.

## Positioning

Fast, cloud-based messaging with powerful groups, bots, and optional privacy modes.

## Customer Model

Free core product; Premium subscription; ecosystem of bots and stickers.

## Excluded / Weak Fit

- Enterprise compliance-first workplaces (relative to Slack/Teams).
- Signed repository collaboration as a primary job-to-be-done.
