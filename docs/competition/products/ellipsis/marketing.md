# Ellipsis Marketing

## Target Customer

Ellipsis markets to GitHub teams and open-source maintainers who want automatic review without adopting a large agent IDE or enterprise platform. Its strongest target is a small-to-mid-size software team that lives in GitHub and wants a second reviewer plus occasional bug-fix automation.

## Positioning

- The headline promise is AI code reviews and bug fixes.
- The product uses simple metrics, YC/social proof, security claims, and testimonials to build trust.
- The value proposition is pragmatic: catch logical bugs and style-guide violations, answer questions, summarize changes, write implementation plans, and generate tested fixes.

## Customer Model

- Public GitHub repositories are free.
- Paid private usage is advertised as a flat per-developer monthly model with unlimited usage.
- Enterprise trust is handled through SOC 2, no data retention claims, and founder/security conversations rather than a large public enterprise matrix.

## Who They Capture

- GitHub-native teams that want low-friction AI review.
- Maintainers who care about review noise and want quiet-mode controls.
- Teams that prefer flat pricing over per-review usage.

## Who They Miss

- Non-GitHub teams and organizations that need first-class multi-forge support.
- Platform teams that require deeply auditable policy management, RBAC, and procurement flows.
- Users who want review, fix, and approval evidence committed as repository-native provenance.
