# Marketing

## Target Customer

Google AI Studio Build targets non-technical creators, Firebase developers, Google Cloud teams, Android/web builders, and enterprises already standardized on Google accounts and Gemini. Antigravity messaging extends the target to professional developers and large teams doing agent-first software work.

## Positioning

- The public position is full-stack app creation from prompts, with Google infrastructure available when the app needs to become real.
- Firebase integration positions Google against Lovable, Bolt, Replit Agent, and other vibe-coding builders by making backend provisioning native.
- Antigravity positions Google for the next stage: agent-first coding with browser, terminal, editor, artifacts, CLI, SDK, and enterprise workflow.

## Customer Model

- AI Studio gives Google an entry point for broad Gemini adoption.
- Firebase services and App Hosting move serious apps into Google Cloud billing and operational tooling.
- Enterprise Antigravity and Gemini positioning create a path toward organizational agent platforms.

## Who They Capture

- Builders who already trust Google identity, Firebase, Gemini, and Google Cloud.
- Teams that want a prompt-to-app path without choosing Supabase, Vercel, or a separate hosted IDE.
- Enterprises looking for a vendor-scale agent platform rather than a startup-only tool.

## Who They Miss

- Teams avoiding Google Cloud lock-in or nervous about product churn.
- Developers who want local-first control, vendor-neutral provenance, or signed Git history before cloud resources are provisioned.
- Users who need transparent cost boundaries before agents create API keys, billing-linked projects, or hosted services.
