# Marketing

## Target Customers

- Large engineering organizations with slow builds, expensive CI, flaky tests, and developer-productivity programs.
- Java, Android, Maven, sbt, and Gradle teams that need build and test acceleration without abandoning existing build systems.
- Platform, DevEx, and engineering-leadership buyers looking for measurable delivery-speed gains and governance as AI increases code volume.

## Positioning

Develocity positions itself as the observability, acceleration, and governance layer for modern software delivery. The current headline connects directly to AI-driven development: generated code increases CI pressure, and Develocity helps teams measure, troubleshoot, optimize, and govern the pipeline.

## Customer Model

- Trial and sales-led enterprise adoption.
- Product value is framed around recovered developer time, CI cost avoidance, faster feedback, failure prevention, and build reliability.
- Supports multiple build systems and CI systems to reach enterprise teams with heterogeneous toolchains.

## Captures

- Organizations where build/test wait time is painful enough to fund developer-productivity tooling.
- Teams that need proof for performance improvements: Build Scan links, dashboards, analytics, and case-study metrics.
- Enterprises that want central control over cache, test acceleration, and build evidence.

## Misses

- Small teams that cannot justify enterprise sales, server setup, or metadata governance.
- Developers who want fully offline diagnostics or are uncomfortable publishing detailed build metadata.
- Non-JVM or non-supported build ecosystems where Develocity's deepest integrations are less compelling.
