# Marketing

## Target Customer

Emergent targets solo builders, non-technical founders, small startups, agencies, and vibe-coding users who want to describe an app and get a working product without learning the full web or mobile development stack.

## Positioning

- The market positioning is agentic app builder: describe the product and let the platform build, test, deploy, and hand off code.
- Third-party coverage emphasizes full-stack apps, landing pages, mobile direction, GitHub integration, and fast prototype-to-deploy.
- Pricing discourse centers on free entry, paid credit packages, and higher tiers for more serious building.

## Customer Model

- The model is credit-metered and tiered, with free or low-cost entry and paid plans for larger builds.
- GitHub integration and deployment are part of the ownership and launch story.
- The product captures demand from people who want app outcomes rather than tools, but who still want access to generated code.

## Who They Capture

- First-time founders who need a demonstrable product quickly.
- Builders who want web and mobile possibilities in one prompt-driven workflow.
- Agencies or freelancers experimenting with fast prototype delivery.
- Users who are willing to trade precise control for speed.

## Who They Miss

- Engineers who want local reproducibility, deterministic tests, and explicit code review before every deploy.
- Buyers who need predictable deployment costs.
- Users burned by credit consumption during failed fixes or tests.
- Regulated teams that need traceable data, security, and deployment evidence.
