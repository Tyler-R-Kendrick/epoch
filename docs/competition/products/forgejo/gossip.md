# Gossip

## What People Say

Self-hosting communities often frame Forgejo as the trust-forward successor path for users worried about Gitea's commercial governance. Many users report that both products are similar in day-to-day features, while Forgejo feels like the brighter long-term bet for community momentum, Codeberg backing, and federation.

## Bug And Friction Themes

- Federation attracts interest but remains experimental, with explicit warnings about missing moderation and access control plus potential breaking changes.
- Some users report Forgejo as simple and satisfying to run; others note setup or OIDC runner integration friction.
- Community debates include governance trust, moderation style, divergence from Gitea, and whether the feature delta justifies migration.
- Because Forgejo is familiar by design, users looking for a materially different code-review or history UX may be disappointed.

## Product Risk For Epoch

Forgejo can own the sovereignty narrative for Git forges. If Epoch's story is only "open and self-hosted," Forgejo already says that in a familiar product category.

## Opportunity For Epoch

Epoch can integrate with Forgejo while providing something Forgejo does not: signed semantic event history, portable actor evidence, and verifiable materialized versions that survive forge migrations or federation churn.
