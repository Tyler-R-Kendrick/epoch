# Marketing

## Target Customers

- Frontend engineers, founders, designers, and product teams building React and Next.js applications.
- Vercel customers who want AI generation inside the same account, deployment, domain, and integration ecosystem.
- Teams that value polished UI scaffolds, shadcn/ui conventions, GitHub sync, and fast preview deployment.

## Positioning

v0 positions itself as an AI pair programmer and app builder for quality UI and end-to-end Vercel shipping. It is not just a component generator anymore; current docs and pricing position it for full-stack apps, AI-powered apps, GitHub sync, Design Mode, and Vercel deployment.

## Customer Model

v0 is sold through Vercel accounts and teams with Free, Team, and Business plans. Plans include monthly credits, daily login credits on paid team plans, centralized billing, collaboration, and business data controls.

## Captures

- Teams already standardizing on Vercel, Next.js, Tailwind CSS, and shadcn/ui.
- Users who care about polished generated UI more than backend breadth.
- Developers who want generated work to move into GitHub and Vercel previews quickly.

## Misses

- Organizations that avoid Vercel dependency or need self-hosted generation and deployment.
- Users who need predictable generation cost before each task.
- Back-end-heavy teams outside the Next.js/Vercel ecosystem.

## Epoch Lessons

v0 shows that generated UI becomes sticky when it is tied to deploy, preview, and team billing. Epoch's opening is portable provenance across tools, not a prettier generator alone.
