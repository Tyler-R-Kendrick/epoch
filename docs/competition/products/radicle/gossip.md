# Gossip

## What People Say

The positive conversation centers on sovereignty and "GitHub without a central owner." The recurring adoption friction is practical: platform support, availability expectations, and the operational meaning of peer-to-peer seeding.

## Bug And Friction Themes

- The product must continually explain repository persistence when nodes go offline.
- Patches and issues are powerful because they are stored in Git, but that model also requires users to learn Radicle-specific collaboration concepts.
- Public issue trackers show ongoing product maturity work typical of a fast-evolving decentralized stack.

## Product Risk For Epoch

Radicle already owns much of the language Epoch might want around local-first sovereign collaboration. Epoch needs sharper differentiation around deterministic signed event replay, CRDT-aware merging, and richer policy semantics.
