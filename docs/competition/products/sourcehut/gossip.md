# Gossip

## What People Say

SourceHut is polarizing in a useful way. Fans praise speed, simplicity, no JavaScript, privacy, and old-school open-source workflow discipline. Critics see mailing-list review as needless friction compared with purpose-built web pull-request tools.

## Bug And Friction Themes

- The public alpha label creates uncertainty about polish, docs completeness, and minor known bugs.
- SourceHut status history includes a 2026 DDoS disruption affecting git.sr.ht and related service behavior.
- Community discussions often debate whether the UI is elegantly minimal or simply too basic.
- Mailing-list collaboration can exclude contributors who are comfortable with GitHub-style web review but not email patch workflows.

## Product Risk For Epoch

SourceHut can capture highly technical users who prefer transparent, scriptable, textual workflows and actively reject AI-heavy or SaaS-heavy product directions.

## Opportunity For Epoch

Epoch can combine SourceHut-like auditability with richer signed history semantics. The key is preserving raw, inspectable event data while giving agent-era teams optional higher-level views.
