# Witness Marketing

## Target Customer

Witness targets DevSecOps teams, platform engineers, release engineers, regulated software producers, and security teams adopting SLSA, in-toto, SBOM, or provenance controls. The buyer is usually the group responsible for proving build integrity, not the casual developer.

## Positioning

Witness positions itself as practical supply-chain evidence collection and verification. The message is less about replacing a forge and more about making CI/CD steps attestable and enforceable.

## Customer Model

- Open-source CLI and verifier workflow.
- Commercial opportunities sit around TestifySec services, enterprise support, integrations, and advisory work.
- Adoption is likely project-by-project inside CI systems before becoming an organization-wide release gate.

## Who They Capture

- Teams already convinced they need attestations but looking for a usable implementation path.
- Organizations that want in-toto-compatible evidence without building a custom provenance pipeline.
- Security leaders who need policy-as-code gates in CI/CD.

## Who They Do Not Capture

- Teams that need a visual source-control workspace or collaborative history UI.
- Developers who want provenance without learning attestation and policy concepts.
- Organizations that are not yet mature enough to invest in CI evidence verification.

## Differentiation Notes

Witness differentiates by staying close to real build commands and enforceable policy. Epoch can differentiate by producing repository-native evidence before CI, then exporting it into Witness-compatible attestation flows.
