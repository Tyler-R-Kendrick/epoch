# Marketing

## Target Customer

Lovable targets founders, product managers, designers, growth teams, internal-tool builders, and small teams that want to build working software without starting in a local IDE. It also targets engineering teams that want an AI-generated app to remain exportable to GitHub, Supabase, Vercel, Netlify, or self-hosting.

## Positioning

- The core position is "describe what you want and get a working full-stack app."
- GitHub integration positions Lovable as more portable and developer-friendly than traditional no-code.
- Supabase integration positions the product as full-stack rather than UI-only.
- Business and Enterprise tiers position Lovable as department-ready with internal publish, SSO, roles, templates, security center, and enterprise GitHub options.

## Customer Model

- Free entry supports experimentation and hosted projects.
- Pro and Business are monthly shared-workspace plans using credits and paid controls.
- Enterprise uses a platform fee based on company size and adds scale, governance, and procurement conversations.

## Who They Capture

- Non-technical builders who value speed over local setup.
- Teams that need a prototype or internal workflow before committing engineering capacity.
- Product teams that want AI UI, backend scaffolding, and GitHub handoff in one place.

## Who They Miss

- Developers who want deterministic local diffs, tests, and review before every change.
- Regulated teams that require signed provenance for prompts, generated files, database policy changes, and deployments.
- Customers who dislike credit accounting or who quickly hit the final 20 percent of app hardening.
