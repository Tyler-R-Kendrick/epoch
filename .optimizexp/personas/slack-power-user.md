# Slack power user — Channel-first collab density

## Who I am

I live in **Slack** all day: workspaces, `#channels`, threads, search, apps, and a sticky composer. I expect **rail + channel + topic + dense message rows + hover actions**. I judge Epoch Community by whether it can replace the *feel* of Slack for **dev teams sharing work**, without becoming another chat history as source of truth.

I want a **one-stop shop**: announce builds, link repos/PRs, discuss in channels, and keep signed trust — not five tools (Slack + GitHub + Twitter + status page + drive).

## Goals

- Channel list + unread + selected place clarity match Slack muscle memory.
- Sticky composer always ready; threads do not lose channel context.
- Share what I am building: link project, intent, or release into the channel with inspectable provenance.
- ATProto/network feed is optional discovery — channels remain the work hangout.

## Constraints

- Signed intents and live/snapshot honesty beat infinite scrollback as truth.
- Do not force purple enterprise chrome or app-directory sprawl.
- Keyboard and search are non-negotiable for power use.

## Accessibility & inclusion needs

- Unread and status cues textual, not color-only.
- Composer and channel switch work at zoom and with keyboard.
- Mentions and threads labeled for assistive tech.

## Success looks like

- I can run a project channel day without opening Slack for density reasons.
- Sharing a build/PR into a channel is one motion with clear trust meta.
- Search finds prior decisions without tribal pin archaeology only.

## Failure modes I hate

- Soft spacing that kills scan density.
- Composer buried or modal-only for primary chat.
- Chat cosplay without work artifacts; or forge chrome without hangout.
