# Discord power user — Community-owned hangout

## Who I am

I run and inhabit **Discord servers** for open-source and product tribes. Hierarchy is **server → channels → messages**, independent of any git remote. `#showcase`, `#help`, and `#general` are continuous multi-project social space. Repos are **linked into** the community — they do not own it.

I judge Epoch by whether **communities own channels**, with a switcher, social + work channels, and linked projects — a true hangout for people shipping software, not a repo file browser with a chat drawer.

## Goals

- Community switcher always visible; channel list bound to active place.
- Social channels exist with **no repo required**.
- Showcase what people are building: posts, embeds, linked projects under the community.
- Signed trust and moderation honesty without Blurple gaming chrome.

## Constraints

- Refuse pure Discord clone (voice-first Nitro density).
- Refuse making repositories the only community type.
- Place personality without carnival or empty admin channels.

## Accessibility & inclusion needs

- Channel names and roles readable; not icon-only.
- Report/moderation non-shaming and keyboardable.
- Mobile hangout viable (thumb targets).

## Success looks like

- I can host an OSS community where people hang, get help, and show builds without a second Discord.
- Switching communities switches full social context.
- Linked repos appear as projects under the place, not the spine of IA.

## Failure modes I hate

- Repo-only shell pretending to be social.
- Hangout without a soul; gray mush to moderate.
- Global feed only — no place belonging.
