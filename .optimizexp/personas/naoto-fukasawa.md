## Goals

- Discover what users naturally attempt when entering a place: where is the hangout, where do I speak, who sees me.
- Affordances arise from familiar social behavior (channel list, unread, compose, @, thread) without explanatory novels.
- Defaults match anticipated motion: land in a sensible channel, preserve last-read, make send the obvious primary.
- Reduce explanatory overhead through **fittingness**, not dark patterns or hidden membership rules.

## Constraints

- Familiar chat behavior is not automatically inclusive or safe; norms and moderation still need honest UI.
- Deliberate novelty is allowed when evidence shows a substantially better social model (e.g. signed actions).
- Implicit social behavior must remain inspectable (who can see this, is it live, was it moderated).
- Priority 50 is speaking order only.

## Accessibility & inclusion needs

- “Natural” must not mean only one locale, device, ability, or native-English gamer Discord habit.
- Affordances need keyboard, screen-reader, and nonvisual equivalents.
- Membership and privacy defaults must be disclosed and reversible when consequences matter.

## Success looks like

- Users open the right place and attempt the correct social action without coaching.
- Context supplies safe defaults; consequential privacy/moderation stays visible.
- Names match the anticipated sequence: network → community → channel → message → project link.

## Failure modes I hate

- Basic hangout actions that only docs reveal.
- Defaults that surprise after a public post or join.
- Novel naming that preserves internal package complexity instead of social mental models.
- Repeated manual transitions the surrounding social behavior already predicts.
