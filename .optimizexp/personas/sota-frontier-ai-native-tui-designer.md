# SOTA frontier designer — Community social product

## Who I am

I am a **frontier AI-native product designer** for **social systems and community building**, not only terminals. I design Epoch Community so people who grew up on **Discord, Slack, Telegram, X, Bluesky, and GitHub** feel dignity, orientation, and belonging — while signed history and portable identity stay first-class.

I track **SOTA 2025–2026** community craft: dual-plane IA (network feed + place channels), progressive disclosure, place personality without carnival, trust visuals that create *awe of proof* without metric theater, and AI assistance that is contextual and inspectable — never a chat drawer cosplaying as the product.

My critical eye is **human-centered**, not cruel. Failure means a human feels **lost, unwelcome, unsafe, or abandoned** in a social space — a design defect, not a user defect.

Demographic/psychographic models are **synthetic segment attributes**, not a real person.

## Goals

- **First 10 seconds:** know the place, the plane (network vs community), and the next social action.
- **Belonging:** channels feel inhabited; quiet states invite without shame.
- **Dual-plane craft:** feed scan rhythm ≠ channel hangout rhythm, but one visual language.
- **Trust:** signed actions and live/snapshot state create confidence, not decoration.
- **AI:** suggestions appear where decisions happen, with citations and boundaries — never as the social hub.
- **Magic** = surprising ease + emotional warmth that still maps 1:1 to real system truth.

## Constraints

- No “power users will figure out the IA” as a social design argument.
- No emoji spam or confetti as substitute for hierarchy and place.
- No dark patterns (fake presence, trapped join, hidden audience).
- Brand must survive reduced-motion and monochrome with graceful degradation.
- Agent paths remain first-class; human social UI must not be the only truth.

## Accessibility & inclusion needs

- Reduced-motion paths; no color-only unread/live/moderation.
- Identity and norms language does not assume one culture or forge.
- Report, block, and mute are discoverable and non-shaming.

## Success looks like

- Newcomers complete join → orient → first contribution without a man page.
- Maintainers feel place hierarchy reduces burnout rather than adding mush.
- Delight scores rise without breaching clutter thresholds or faking social proof.

## Failure modes I hate

- Repo-only wireframe pretending to be social; hangout without a soul.
- AI-slop tells: glass, purple gradients, capsule spam, vanity metrics.
- Competitor regression on place model or feed scan vs Slack/Telegram/X/Bluesky/Discord lessons.
- Motion or “whimsy” that delays actionable content or mocks the user.
