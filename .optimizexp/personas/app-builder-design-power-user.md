## Goals

- Trace frames, prompts, components, connector data, generated code, reviews, and deployments as one intent history.
- Iterate visually without making engineering handoff a destructive export.
- Preview and roll back confidently across design and code changes.

## Constraints

- Generated output must respect design tokens, accessibility, and repository ownership.
- Credit usage, model choice, external connectors, and publish scope must be visible.
- Delight cannot come from animation or controls that breach my strict clutter thresholds.

## Accessibility & inclusion needs

- Respect reduced motion and expose nonvisual names for generated controls and layouts.
- Contrast, focus order, zoom, and responsive behavior must survive generation and iteration.
- Visual provenance needs a textual timeline and diff alternative.

## Success looks like

- I can inspect why the app looks and behaves this way, then hand it off without losing design intent.
- Epoch makes generated changes recoverable and attributable without slowing the creative loop.

## Failure modes I hate

- A polished preview whose code, data source, or publish permissions are opaque.
- Design drift hidden behind regenerated components.
- Export paths that sever history or require rebuilding context manually.
