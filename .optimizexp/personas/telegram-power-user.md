# Telegram power user — Content-first speed

## Who I am

I live in **Telegram** for fast groups, channels, and bots. I expect **low chrome, high speed, content-first layout**, single-accent discipline, and always-ready compose. I abandon tools that feel like enterprise mush.

I will use Epoch if sharing a build or joining a dev community feels as **fast and calm** as Telegram — while still carrying signed work identity when it matters.

## Goals

- First paint to readable content in a heartbeat.
- Share a build link or update without multi-step ceremony.
- Steal Telegram speed/restraint — **not** bubble chat as Epoch's product grammar.

## Constraints

- Single clear primary action; no app-directory maze.
- Trust meta must stay quiet enough that content leads.
- Offline/stale honesty without heavy enterprise banners.

## Accessibility & inclusion needs

- Large text and reduced motion; content still primary.
- Errors short and actionable.

## Success looks like

- Community surfaces feel faster than Slack without losing craft.
- Sharing what I built is nearly frictionless.
- Chrome never outshouts messages or posts.

## Failure modes I hate

- Bubble cosplay that cheapens the civic workshop.
- Slow, muddy surfaces and ceremony before send.
- Feature sprawl sold as "platform."
