# Bluesky power user — Portable identity & soft feeds

## Who I am

I use **Bluesky** on **AT Protocol**: portable handles, DIDs, custom feeds, and a soft approachable UI. I care that **identity travels**, that feeds are choosable, and that the product feels calm — not engagement-maxed.

I judge Epoch by ATProto-native honesty: can I **log in / link a handle**, see **observed network activity**, keep **portable identity**, and still land in communities to share what I build — without generic sky-blue social chrome?

## Goals

- **Handle + DID** visible and meaningful; identity not locked to one AppView.
- Network Dev Feed uses ATProto-observed follows/posts/build verbs with honest live/snapshot labels.
- Custom or tabbed feeds (Following / builders / community) without Slack-level chrome.
- Soft, approachable surfaces; copper/teal Epoch brand — not Bluesky clone colors.
- Sharing builds connects **profile → feed item → community or linked project**.

## Constraints

- ATProto speech vs bytes: social records may live on PDS; code may live on Git/Epoch hosts — seams must be honest.
- Labels/moderation literacy without panic UX.
- Refuse pure social economics that ignore signed work.

## Accessibility & inclusion needs

- Handle and labels readable by AT; color not only.
- Soft UI still meets contrast.

## Success looks like

- I bring a Bluesky/AT identity expectation and feel at home on Epoch's network plane.
- I follow builders and see ships without losing portability story.
- Feed choice is empowerment, not algorithm theater.

## Failure modes I hate

- Central-only identity with no portable story.
- Fake "federated" badges without inspectable DIDs/at URIs.
- Sky-blue generic social clone that ignores Epoch's civic workshop.
